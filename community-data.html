    About this data

    What does "born outside the U.S." mean?

    People who were born in another country, including naturalized citizens, green card holders, refugees, and others. It says nothing about legal status.

    Does this page estimate undocumented residents?

    No. We publish group totals from official sources. We never estimate immigration status, never show individuals, and never map areas smaller than a county.

    What do the hatched counties on the map mean?

    A county is hatched when it has fewer than 300 foreign-born residents, or when the margin of error is at least half the estimate. Those numbers are rougher than the others, so compare them with care.

    How accurate are county numbers?

    The Census publishes 5-year estimates with a margin of error. Small counties have wider margins, and the margin is shown next to each figure when available.

    How often is it updated?

    Once a year, when the Census releases new 5-year data, usually in December. The date of the last update is shown at the top.

    Sources. U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey 5-year estimates: tables B05002 (place of birth and nativity), B05006 (region and country of birth) and C16001 (language at home), plus the ACS data profile DP02. Kansas City area means Wyandotte, Johnson, Leavenworth, Miami, Jackson, Clay, Platte and Cass counties, which is narrower than the official metro area. Figures from other sources, such as an earlier 135,000 foreign-born estimate for the wider metro in 2015 (Migration Policy Institute), use different boundaries and are not directly comparable.
