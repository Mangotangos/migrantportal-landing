How It Works

We connect people who can help
with people who need help.

Right now, in real-time — bridging migrants and their families with volunteers, faith communities, and community organizations across your city.

Join the Network Request a Demo

The MigrantPortal Process

👨‍👩‍👧
Step 1
1 Uncovering Needs

A migrant in need is identified by a trusted local organization

Faith communities and nonprofits serve on the front lines, and they see needs up close.

Every community has churches, nonprofits, and community centers that walk alongside migrants and their families daily. These navigators have first-hand knowledge of the critical and urgent needs that stand between a family and stability — from school enrollment to housing to basic necessities. MigrantPortal gives them a trusted channel to act on what they see.
📋
Step 2
2 Submitting Needs

The organization verifies the need, then MigrantPortal reviews it

Every need is checked twice before it is published: once by the organization, once by our team.

Navigators, the faith communities and nonprofits who know the family personally, verify each need before they submit it to MigrantPortal. Then a MigrantPortal administrator reviews the request to make sure it is accurate, legitimate, and genuinely important to the family's wellbeing. Only approved needs are published. This layer of trust protects families and ensures responders can give with confidence. Family privacy is protected throughout.
📣
Step 3
3 Sharing Needs

MigrantPortal alerts nearby faith communities and volunteers

Once a need is approved, alerts go out by email and app notification to responders near the family.

Once a need is approved, MigrantPortal notifies faith communities, volunteers, and partner organizations within close proximity. Local faith communities take a leading role in mobilizing their response teams to confirm they can meet the need — whether it's a tangible item, financial support, or relational care. Community organizations and individual donors can also respond, ensuring no need goes unmet due to a lack of resources.
🤝
Step 4
4 Meeting Needs

A volunteer is connected with the organization, and the family is helped

Real people show up. Real needs get met. Families experience genuine community care.

When a faith community or volunteer offers to meet a need, the organization that submitted it is notified and can chat with the volunteer in the app to arrange the details. Gifts and help go to that organization, which gets them to the family. When the need is met, the organization marks it fulfilled, and the volunteer and the MigrantPortal team are notified. Every met need is a real story of a family receiving dignity, safety, and hope, and the cycle of care continues for the next family in need.
