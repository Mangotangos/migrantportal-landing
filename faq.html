Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using MigrantPortal to help migrants and their families.

Getting Started

What is MigrantPortal? ▾
MigrantPortal connects immigrants, refugees, and their families in need with volunteers, faith communities, and organizations who want to help. Navigators submit verified needs on behalf of the families they know; responders like you step in to meet those needs.
Who can use MigrantPortal? ▾
Anyone can sign up. Navigators are faith communities and nonprofits who verify and submit needs on behalf of families they serve. Responders are volunteers, churches, or individuals who help fulfill those needs. Every new account is reviewed by a MigrantPortal administrator before it is activated, so approval can take a short while.
Is MigrantPortal free? Where do I get the app? ▾
Yes. MigrantPortal is free for everyone. You can use the website, or download the app from the App Store or Google Play.
Is MigrantPortal available in Spanish? ▾
Partly. The home page, Browse Requests, Find Help, Contact, sign-in, registration, and need pages have an EN | ES switch in the header. Other pages, including this FAQ, are English only for now.
Can I help if I don't live near a family in need? ▾
Yes. Many requests are marked Can help remotely, such as funding, translation, or online help. Use that filter on the Browse Requests page. If no open needs are near your ZIP code yet, you can join the waitlist and we will let you know when families in your area are on the platform.

Responding to Needs

How do I respond to a need? ▾
Open Browse Requests, filter by category, distance, or remote help, and tap a need to see the details. Then choose how you want to help: provide an item or service, volunteer your time, or fund the need. The navigator who submitted it is notified right away.
What happens after I respond? ▾
The navigator reviews your response and coordinates with you in the in-app chat. You will get a notification when they accept. Each need page shows a "Your next step" card, and when it is time to hand something over, the navigator can share the drop-off site and hours with accepted responders. Once the help is delivered and recorded, the need is marked fulfilled.
What types of needs can I help with? ▾
Needs fall into 13 categories: housing, food, clothing, furniture, transportation, employment, legal, medical, education, translation, childcare, financial, and other. Filter by category to find what matches your skills or resources.
How do I get alerts for needs I care about? ▾
Go to Profile & Settings and open "Needs I'm alerted about." Pick the need types you want and how far from your ZIP code you want to hear about them (organizations choose the distance from their own ZIP). Alerts arrive by email and app notification.
What does "Partially Met" mean? ▾
A partially met need has received some help but still requires more. For example, if a family needed $300 and received $150, the need stays open so other responders can cover the rest. Items are tracked one by one, so you can see exactly what is still missing.

Donations & Funding

What's the difference between Providing and Funding a request? ▾
If you choose to Provide, you personally obtain or donate the item or service. If you choose to Fund, you make a monetary gift toward that specific need, and the navigator uses it to meet the need on the family's behalf.
When I fund a request, where does the money go? ▾
You choose an amount on the need page and pay through our secure checkout. Gifts are tied to that one need and held in escrow. The navigator then requests disbursement and uses the funds for the need, such as paying a bill or buying the item. Funds are tracked need by need, not pooled.
Is my gift tax-deductible? ▾
Not yet. MigrantPortal Inc. is in the process of registering as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Until that status is granted, gifts fund family needs directly but are not tax-deductible. See the Donate page for details.

Volunteering & Shifts

How do volunteer shifts work? ▾
Organizations post volunteer shifts for ongoing events such as food distributions or ESL classes. Sign up from the Shifts page after you log in. Your hours are logged once a shift is marked complete.
What am I responsible for when I respond? ▾
You commit to following through on what you offered, whether that is delivering items, providing a service, or funding a need. If your situation changes, please tell the navigator promptly in the chat so the family isn't left without help.

Trust & Verification

Where do MigrantPortal requests come from? ▾
Requests are submitted by approved navigators, the faith communities and nonprofits who know the family and verify the need first. A MigrantPortal administrator then reviews every need before it goes live.
How do you keep families safe? ▾
Every account, navigator, and need is reviewed by an administrator. Family names, addresses, and contact details are never shown publicly. Read our child safety standards and Privacy Policy for more.

Privacy & Safety

How is my personal information used? ▾
Your name and organization are shared with the need's navigator when you respond, so they can coordinate the handoff. Your contact details are never shared publicly. Review our full Privacy Policy.
Is family data kept confidential? ▾
Yes. MigrantPortal does not display family names, addresses, or contact information publicly. Only the navigator and accepted responders can see coordination details. Navigators must agree to a data confidentiality policy at sign-up.

Support

I have more questions. Who do I contact? ▾
Tap the chat on any need to message the submitting navigator, or visit our Contact page. For platform issues, email support@migrantportal.com.
