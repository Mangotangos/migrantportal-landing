How to Volunteer and Help Immigrants in Kansas City

Local organizations that welcome volunteers, and what each one asks you to do.

See Real Needs How It Works

Start Here

Pick what fits your time

One day or one weekend: help set up an apartment for a newly arrived family or volunteer at a community event. A regular weekly commitment: tutor English, mentor a family, or give rides to appointments. Flexible: join a conversation practice group, or donate winter clothing and household items.

Everything below was checked against each organization's own page on November 21, 2026. Volunteer needs change, so contact them before you plan your day.

Where to Volunteer

Catholic Charities of Northeast Kansas, Refugee & Immigration (Kansas City, KS)

Open to individuals, families with children, youth groups and corporate groups. Roles include welcoming new refugees (setting up apartments, greeting families at the airport, helping with transportation, supplying culturally familiar food), tutoring, mentoring and teaching, and volunteering at events such as World Refugee Day and Welcoming Week.

Time: one-time commitments of a day or a weekend, or ongoing weekly, every-other-week and monthly. Some mentoring programs ask for a year. English and citizenship classes usually meet once a week in the evening, and you do not need formal teaching training.

Volunteer application and details

Della Lamb Community Services, Refugee & Immigrant Services (Kansas City, MO)

Volunteers help in the digital literacy lab, tutor English as a second language, or drive newcomers to appointments. Companies, faith groups, and families or friends can volunteer as a team, built around a single event or an ongoing commitment. You can create a volunteer profile or ask for a tour of their facilities.

Della Lamb volunteer page

KC for Refugees (greater Kansas City)

Welcomes people who want to support refugee families. Ways to help include mentorship (visiting and spending time with a family to offer friendship and orient them to life here), finding interpreters for special circumstances, collecting household goods and furniture, and clothing drives. Winter hats, jackets and boots are listed as especially needed as seasons change. Upcoming events are posted on their Facebook page.

kcforrefugees.org

Kansas City Public Library, RISE (Kansas City, MO)

The library's Let's Talk conversation group pairs learners with volunteer tutors for English practice, with at least three sessions a week. See the library's RISE volunteer page for how to sign up.

RISE volunteer page

International Rescue Committee, Kansas City office (Kansas City, KS)

The IRC's Kansas City office is at 402 State Ave, Kansas City, KS 66101, phone 913-558-1659. Its page links to current volunteer opportunities.
