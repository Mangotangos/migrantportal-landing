Mission Statement

Official Mission

To dignify the migrant experience by connecting families in need with communities ready to respond — where help meets hope.

MigrantPortal is a free platform and nonprofit initiative that connects migrant families in crisis with the volunteers, churches, and organizations in their community who are ready to help. It launched in Kansas City in 2026.

Founder

Nirmal Samuel

Nirmal Samuel is the founder of MigrantPortal, a technology professional based in Kansas City, Missouri. Drawing on his own experience as an immigrant and a commitment to the dignity of every person, he built MigrantPortal around one question: why is it so hard for someone who wants to help to find someone who needs it? He leads the platform's technology, partnerships, and growth.

Media inquiries: support@migrantportal.com
