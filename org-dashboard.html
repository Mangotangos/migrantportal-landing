How It Works
Everything your organization needs to know to use this dashboard
From need to help, step by step
  1. Invite your teamOpen Team Members and share your invite code. Staff and volunteers who sign up with it join your organization.
  2. Set your drop-off siteAlso in Team Members. It tells responders where to bring items. Only you and the responder whose offer you accept can see it.
  3. Submit a needClick Submit a Need and describe what a family needs. The family's name and your private notes stay with your organization.
  4. We review itNew needs show Awaiting Admin Approval until our team checks them. Once approved, the need goes live.
  5. Responders offer helpResponders near the family are notified. Their offers appear on the need's page and in your Recent Activity.
  6. Accept an offerOpen the need and click Accept offer and open chat. Use the chat to arrange pickup, drop-off, or a time.
  7. Mark it fulfilledWhen the family has the help, open the need and click Mark fulfilled. Everyone involved is notified.
  8. Help other families tooYour team can also respond to needs posted by others. See Needs Near You on the Dashboard or Browse Needs.
What's on your dashboard
  • Total Needs SubmittedEvery need your organization has posted, all time.
  • Open NeedsYour needs that are live and still waiting to be fulfilled.
  • Open Near YouNeeds posted by others within 25 miles that your team could help with.
  • Team MembersPeople who joined with your invite code. Click it to manage your team.
  • Needs Near YouThe closest open needs. Click one to see details and offer help.
  • Recent NeedsYour latest submissions and their status. "See all" opens Submitted Needs.
  • Recent ActivityNew offers, accepted helpers, and fulfilled needs. The full list is under Activity.
What the labels mean
  • Awaiting ApprovalWaiting for our team to approve it. Responders can't see it yet.
  • OpenNot fulfilled yet. Open the need to see offers.
  • UrgentHow soon the family needs help: Low, Medium, High, or Urgent.
  • FulfilledDone. Everyone involved was notified.
Privacy
  • Responders seeThe need, category, urgency, and general area (city), so they can decide to help.
  • Only your org seesThe family's name and your private notes.
  • After you acceptThe responder you accept can chat with you and see your drop-off site.
